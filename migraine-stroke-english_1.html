Grammar: should / can / must

Выбери правильный модальный глагол для клинических рекомендаций и выводов.

must / mustn'tстрогая необходимость или прямой запрет (протокол, безопасность).
should / shouldn'tсовет, рекомендация (не строго обязательно).
can / can'tвозможность, способность или (не)доказанность.
1. According to the clinical protocol, a neurologist always assess the risk of stroke in patients with migraine with aura.
2. Without a large sample the results won't be significant, so researchers enroll enough participants.
3. You ignore a sudden, severe headache — it can be associated with a stroke.
4. Doctors follow high-risk patients for several years to detect new strokes early, even though it isn't obligatory in every case.
5. According to the guidelines, GPs refer patients with migraine with aura to a neurologist for further assessment.
6. One small study prove that migraine confers an increased risk of stroke — more research is needed.
7. If a patient has several risk factors, the hazard ratio for stroke be significantly higher than in the general population.
8. Lifestyle changes alone always guarantee a lower risk of stroke — medication may also be necessary.

05

Speaking: mini case presentation

Представь, что объясняешь однокурснику (или пациенту), который не разбирается в неврологии: что такое мигрень, что такое аура, что такое инсульт, и как вообще учёные изучают связь между болезнями. Расскажи мини-историю (1–2 минуты), стараясь использовать как можно больше выражений из списка ниже.

1
Что такое мигрень

"Migraine is a neurological condition that causes intense, often one-sided headaches, sometimes with nausea and sensitivity to light."

2
Что такое аура

"Some patients also experience an aura — visual or sensory symptoms, such as flashing lights or numbness, that appear shortly before or during an attack."

3
Что такое инсульт

"A stroke happens when blood flow to part of the brain is interrupted, and it can damage brain tissue within minutes. It's important to know the difference between a prevalent and an incident stroke."

4
Как изучают такие болезни

"To understand the risk of stroke, researchers usually enroll a large group of participants and follow them for several years. At study baseline, they assess each person's health and risk factors."

5
Что показывают такие исследования

"According to several studies, migraine with aura is associated with an elevated risk of stroke — although, contrary to expectations, scientists still don't fully understand this connection."
