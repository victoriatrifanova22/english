Reel Two

Improv Time

Pick one scene (or try all three) and act it out together. Stay in character — the prompts are just there to keep the conversation rolling.

Scene 1 · Interior — Your Apartment — Evening

Comforting a Broken Heart

Your close friend just went through a painful breakup. She's not sleeping well, barely eating, and keeps canceling plans. Tonight she finally came over.

Try to use: should, must

  • What should you say to her first?
  • What should she avoid doing right now?
  • What must she do to start feeling better?
  • Is there anything you shouldn't say, even with good intentions?
Scene 2 · Interior — A Phone Call — Late Evening

Heading Toward Burnout

Your friend has been working late every night this month. She sounds exhausted on the phone and admits she can't remember the last day she didn't think about work.

Try to use: should, must

  • What should she do first thing tomorrow?
  • What must change so she doesn't burn out completely?
  • Should she talk to her manager? Why or why not?
  • What advice would you give her about setting boundaries?
Scene 3 · Interior — A Cafe — Afternoon

Running Away or Starting Over?

Your friend just announced he's moving abroad to "start fresh." You suspect he isn't running toward something new — he's running away from himself. Explain your view, weighing both sides of the decision.

Try to use: however, though, although, on the one hand, on the other hand, by the way, also

  • What are the possible benefits of moving to a new country?
  • What are the risks or downsides of this decision?
  • Why might moving abroad not solve the real problem?
  • What would you suggest he do instead, or alongside the move?

Reel Three

Title Cards

Five quotes on love, travel, and nature — some comforting, some a little controversial. Read each one aloud, then talk it through.

On Relationships
“Each man kills the thing he loves.”
— Oscar Wilde

Discuss

  • Do you agree that people sometimes damage the relationships they care about most? How?
  • Is this quote too pessimistic, or does it hold some truth?
On Relationships
“There is always some madness in love. But there is also always some reason in madness.”
— Friedrich Nietzsche

Discuss

  • Have you ever done something "irrational" because of love or a strong connection?
  • Can love ever be completely logical? Should it be?
On Travel
“Life is either a daring adventure or nothing at all.”
— Helen Keller

Discuss

  • Do you agree, or is this too extreme — can a quiet, safe life still be a good one?
  • What's the most "daring" thing you'd like to do someday?
On Nature
“Adopt the pace of nature: her secret is patience.”
— Ralph Waldo Emerson

Discuss

  • Does modern life make it harder to be patient? Why?
  • What's one thing nature has taught you, directly or indirectly?
On Nature
“In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks.”
— John Muir

Discuss

  • Do you agree spending time outdoors gives you more than you expect?
  • How does time in nature compare to a "dopamine detox" we talked about earlier?
