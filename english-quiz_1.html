5 Quotes to Reflect On

Just like the extra scene after the credits in a superhero movie — read the quote and discuss it out loud in English using the ideas below.

Scene 01 / 05
"Comparison is the thief of joy."
— Theodore Roosevelt
  • Do you agree that comparing yourself to others usually makes people less happy? Why or why not?
  • Describe a moment when comparing yourself to someone else affected your mood.
  • What is one way to measure your own progress without comparing yourself to others?
Scene 02 / 05
"The only way to do great work is to love what you do."
— Steve Jobs
  • Do you think you have to love a job to be excellent at it, or can skill alone be enough?
  • What is one activity you love doing so much that time seems to disappear?
  • If money were not a factor, what work would you choose, and why?
Scene 03 / 05
"It always seems impossible until it's done."
— Nelson Mandela
  • Think of a goal that once felt impossible to you. What changed your mind?
  • How do you usually react when a task looks too difficult at first?
  • What is one "impossible" goal you would like to try this year?
Scene 04 / 05
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."
— Eleanor Roosevelt
  • What role do you think belief plays in reaching a dream, compared to hard work or luck?
  • Describe a dream you have for the next five years.
  • Who is someone you admire because they followed their dream despite difficulties?
Scene 05 / 05
"Fear is only as deep as the mind allows."
— Japanese proverb
  • Can you think of a fear that turned out to be smaller than you expected once you faced it?
  • What is a strategy you use to manage fear or anxiety before a big event, like an exam?
  • Do you think all fears can be overcome with the right mindset, or are some fears useful to keep?
