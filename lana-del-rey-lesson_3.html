Reading: her story

while-reading task

Elizabeth Grant, known as Lana Del Rey, has become a very famous pop singer. Over the years, she has created a unique musical world full of romance and sadness.

She was born in New York in 1985. As a teenager, she started singing and learned to play the guitar. At first, she performed in small clubs under her real name, Lizzy Grant. She released some early songs, but she didn't become popular right away.

Her life changed completely in 2011. She made a simple music video for her song "Video Games" and uploaded it to YouTube. The song immediately became very popular on the internet. In 2012, she released her big album Born to Die. It was a great success all over the world.

Since that time, Lana has released nine studio albums. She has worked with famous artists like The Weeknd and Taylor Swift. Over the years, she has changed her music style several times. She has won many music awards and has influenced younger singers, like Billie Eilish. Lana Del Rey has proven that she is a very talented and special artist.

Grammar focus

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple

A completed action at a specific moment in the past. The exact time is stated or clear from the context.

  • She was born in 1985.
  • In 2011, she uploaded the video.
  • At first, she performed in small clubs.

Present Perfect

An action or period connected to the present: a life experience, an unfinished period of time, or a result visible now. No exact time is given.

  • She has released nine albums (so far).
  • She has worked with The Weeknd.
  • She has influenced younger singers.
💡 Signal words: Past Simple → in [year], at first, when she was young. Present Perfect → since, so far, over the years, several times, never/ever.
