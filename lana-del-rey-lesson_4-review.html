Task 3

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

recap with new sentences

Past Simple

A finished action at a specific, stated moment in the past (in 2012, at first, when she was young).

Present Perfect

An action or period connected to now: experience, an unfinished period, a visible result (so far, over the years, several times).
