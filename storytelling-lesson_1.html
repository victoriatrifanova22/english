01

Soundcheck

Before the main set, every show needs a soundcheck. Talk about these questions with your teacher.

  • What solitary activity do you like best, and why? cooking · gardening · reading · drawing · sewing · or something else
  • Which countries do you think have a high level of "othering" (treating a group of people as different or lesser)? Why do you think that happens there?
  • Which works of art — a song, a film, a painting, a book — have given you a truly profound experience?
  • What could you do for a whole day without ever getting bored? Has that actually happened to you?
02

Behind the Lyrics

The rule

-ed adjectives describe how a person feels.

"I was bored during the opening act."

-ing adjectives describe the person, thing, or situation that causes that feeling.

"The opening act was boring."
